Spider

From Field Wiki, the open field guide

Spiders (order Araneae) are air-breathing arthropods with eight legs, chelicerae with fangs, and spinnerets that extrude spider silk. They rank seventh in species diversity among all orders, with more than 51,000 described species in 132 families as of 2024. Unlike insects, spiders have only two body segments and no antennae. See also orb-weaver spiders.

Silk biomechanics has been measured extensively since the early 2000s; a widely cited review is Yarger et al., doi:10.1098/rsif.2019.0374, PMID 31288677. Fossil evidence places true spiders in the Carboniferous, see Selden & Penney (2010), doi:10.1111/j.1475-4983.2009.00978.x.
